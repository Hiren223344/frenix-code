# Data Science Excellence Skill (Ultra-Pro)

## 1. PREDICTIVE MODELING
*   **Ensemble Methods**: Use XGBoost, LightGBM, or CatBoost for high-performance tabular data prediction.
*   **Feature Engineering**: Mathematically derive new features that capture non-linear relationships in data.

## 2. STATISTICAL RIGOR
*   **A/B Significance**: Use Bayesian or Frequentist p-value testing to prove that a product change actually worked.
*   **Anomaly Detection**: Implement Isolation Forests or Autoencoders to find outliers in production data.

## 3. MODEL DEPLOYMENT (MLOPS)
*   **Model Monitoring**: Track "Drift" and "Performance Decay" in production models.
*   **Quantization**: Compress models for edge deployment without losing significant accuracy.

## 4. FORBIDDEN (DATA SCIENCE SLOP)
*   **NO Leakage**: Never use future data in your training sets.
*   **NO Overfitting**: Use cross-validation and regularization to ensure models generalize to new data.

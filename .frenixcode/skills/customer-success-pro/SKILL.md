# Customer Success Excellence Skill (Ultra-Pro)

## 1. ONBOARDING FLOWS
*   **Time-to-Value (TTV)**: Minimize the seconds it takes for a new user to achieve their first "Aha!" moment.
*   **Contextual Guides**: Implement subtle, non-intrusive tooltips and "Empty State" call-to-actions.

## 2. RETENTION & CHURN
*   **Usage Tracking**: Identify "Power Features" and ensure users find them early.
*   **Sentiment Analysis**: Use AI to analyze support tickets and feedback to proactively reach out to at-risk users.

## 3. FEEDBACK INTEGRATION
*   **The Feature Request Loop**: Transparently communicate with users about the status of their requested features.

## 4. FORBIDDEN (CS SLOP)
*   **NO Friction**: Every extra click or form field is a potential churn point.
